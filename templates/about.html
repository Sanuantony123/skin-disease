About SkinCare AI Platform

An engineering and deep learning decision-support prototype exploring computer vision for preliminary photographic skin screening across 23 dermatological disease categories.

System Architecture & Engineering Methodology

Multi-tiered validation, inference, and evidence generation

1. Non-Skin Validation Guard

Dual-stage heuristic and convolutional pre-screening rejects non-dermatological objects (vehicles, pets, food, landscapes, documents) prior to clinical evaluation.

2. MobileNetV2 Inference

Transfer learning model with depthwise separable convolutions maps subtle epidermal texture, color variance, and lesion margins at 224x224 RGB resolution.

3. Clinical Knowledge Engine

Generates comprehensive management summaries, covering root etiology, prevention guidelines, daily skin regimens, prescription therapies, and urgent red flags.

Mandatory Clinical & Regulatory Notice

SkinCare AI is an educational computer science research project. It does NOT possess FDA or CE certification as an active medical diagnostic device. Digital photographic classification cannot replace the holistic diagnostic process of a board-certified dermatologist, which incorporates palpation, dermoscopy, medical history, and biopsies. Always consult a licensed clinician for medical concerns.
